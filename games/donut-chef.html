📖 How to play Donut Chef

Goal: Finish with the most victory points (🍩 recipes) in your deck when either the Premium donut pile is empty or any 3 supply piles run out. Ties go to the chef who took fewer turns!

  1. Action phase: Play 1 blue action card from your hand (some grant extra actions, cards, buys, or coins), or tap Skip actions to move to buying.
  2. Buy phase: Play gold ingredient cards for coins (or tap any affordable card in the supply shop to auto-spend ingredients) and purchase cards into your discard pile.
  3. Cleanup: Played and remaining hand cards discard automatically, and you draw 5 fresh cards.
